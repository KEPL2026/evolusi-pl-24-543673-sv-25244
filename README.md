# Archive of Growth

**Archive of Growth** is an interactive digital archive that documents a learning journey through experiences, projects, and personal growth.

## Background

Learning is not only about the final result. The process behind it also contains valuable experiences, challenges, mistakes, and lessons that are worth remembering.

As learning progresses, these experiences can become scattered across different files, repositories, notes, and projects. This makes it difficult to look back and understand how a person has grown over time.

**Archive of Growth** was created as a digital space to collect and organize those experiences into an interactive learning journey.

Instead of presenting learning only as a collection of finished projects, this project focuses on documenting the journey behind them.

## Concept

The application uses an **archive and book** metaphor.

- **Archive of Growth** — the overall collection of the learning journey.
- **Shelf** — a broad area of the journey, such as Academic, Projects, Organization, or Research.
- **Book** — a specific experience or journey within a shelf.
- **Chapter** — a section of a book.
- **Step** — an individual learning or progress step.
- **Journey Mode** — an interactive narrative that presents the story, experiences, screenshots, code, and lessons learned.

The structure can be represented as:

Archive of Growth
└── Shelf
    └── Book
        └── Chapter
            └── Step
                └── Journey Story
